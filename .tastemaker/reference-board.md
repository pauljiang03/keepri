## Sources and actual observations

- Primary user-selected reference: https://www.virio.ai/. Retrieved current HTML, CSS and social preview. Observed large regular sans-serif type, a repeated ATTENTION tile composition in the source, warm cream/ink/olive/clay colors, concise navigation, bracketed numbered process stages, and an explicit thesis section. The public social image was visually inspected and its palette extracted. Browser screenshots were unavailable, so no claim is made to have reviewed its rendered motion.
- Adjacent research product: https://arcprize.org/. Retrieved through web search and fetch. Its page organizes the argument around interactive tasks, human performance, and concrete research artifacts. Borrow the specificity of task descriptions, not its data or results.
- Adjacent company: https://www.pi.website/. Retrieved current page text. A terse mission statement leads into named research posts. Borrow the short company introduction and direct path to substantive work. No claims about its visual style from text-only retrieval.
- Cultural source: the KeepRI app's physical-board-like circles, diamonds, and square cells, visually inspected in the native captures. Borrow those forms in the opening ribbon.
- Interface system: the existing React stack, native HTML semantics, existing Lucide icons, and the installed shadcn catalog. No registry component was necessary for this static reading surface.
- Anti-reference: the previous version's repeated italic headlines, installation CTAs, bright lime product panel, release dates, and repeated marketing slogans.

## Direction contract

Thesis: independent reasoning deserves an enjoyable consumer home, with a separately consented research business supporting that mission.
First viewport: broad two-line thesis, a compact explanation, one anchor action, and a cropped full-width game-piece ribbon.
System: muted olive, warm paper, regular sans-serif, restrained section transitions, real native product captures.
Risk: resembling a benchmark vendor or overstating current research operations. Keep the consumer experience central and mark the research program as in development.
